Our story

In 2009, Ruth Nakato opened a small shop on Market Street. Neighbours came in for sugar and stayed for the news. Soon they were asking for more: rice, soap, school books.

Today Marigold has three branches and 38 people on the team. We still weigh rice by hand if you ask. We still remember how you take your tea.

  1. 2009

    Central opens

    One counter, one scale and a kettle that was always on.

  2. 2015

    Northgate opens

    Parents asked for a shop near the school. We listened.

  3. 2021

    Lakeside opens

    Our biggest branch, with a bakery corner.

  4. 2026

    Marigold goes online

    Now you can shop from your phone. You are looking at it.
